# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers of AgentOS pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes:
- Using welcoming, clear, and respectful technical communication.
- Accepting constructive engineering feedback with professionalism.
- Focusing on verifiable facts, security, and maintainability.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information or credentials without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, backdoors, or deceptive metrics.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `dandisetiawan.co@gmail.com`. Maintainers review reports promptly and take fair corrective action, up to removal from project spaces.
